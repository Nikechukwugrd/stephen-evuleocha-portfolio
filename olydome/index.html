What this demo is proving

The portfolio story is not “this predicts pumps.” It is “I can formulate, engineer, and validate a noisy market decision system.”

Signal separation

Opportunity, risk, and data confidence are modeled separately so uncertain inputs do not masquerade as strong forecasts.

Executable outcomes

Evaluation should use realizable exits after depth, slippage, and fees—not headline price or market-cap moves.

False-positive control

Precision and calibration matter more than generating a large number of alerts in a market full of adversarial and low-quality assets.

Research reproducibility

Features, thresholds, timestamps, model versions, and outcomes should be persisted so every claim can be retested.
