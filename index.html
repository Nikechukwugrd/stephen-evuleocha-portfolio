Olydome research framing

Turn “find a token” into testable market research.

1. Hypothesis

Can early liquidity growth, concentration, order-flow acceleration, and exit capacity predict survival or extreme drawdown?

2. Features

Liquidity depth, holder concentration, transaction imbalance, unique participant growth, age, velocity, and executable sellability.

3. Validation

Time-split evaluation, precision/recall, calibration, realized returns after slippage, maximum drawdown, and failure-case review.

4. Engineering

Asynchronous ingestion, durable queues, columnar analytics, relational metadata, API delivery, observability, and reproducible experiments.

Research direction

Questions worth publishing.

01

Liquidity Growth, Wallet Concentration & Short-Horizon Survival

Empirical study of whether early microstructure variables predict survival, liquidity decay, or severe drawdown in newly issued digital assets.

02

Displayed vs. Executable Returns in Illiquid Markets

Measure the gap between marked-to-market P&L and realizable P&L after slippage, depth, fees, and exit constraints.

03

Model Confidence Under Regime Shift

Track calibration drift and false-positive rates as market conditions change, emphasizing confidence-aware decision systems.
